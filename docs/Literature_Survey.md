# Literature Survey

The literature survey contains the review of fifteen recent research papers published between 2023 and 2026 related to:

- Green AI
- Sustainable Cloud Computing
- Carbon Footprint Estimation
- Carbon Intelligence Analytics
- Digital Carbon Passports
- Cloud Resource Optimization
- Digital Product Passports

The detailed literature survey and comparative analysis are maintained in this document and form the basis for identifying the research gap addressed by the proposed framework.
